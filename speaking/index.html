Speaking

Speaker, chair and moderator on government, cities and capital.

Phil Broadhead OBE speaks, chairs and moderates at conferences across the Gulf and beyond, on how governments and investors turn strategy into results.

Invite Phil Download speaker kit

Phil spent 15 years at the heart of UK government, attending Cabinet and serving as Leader of Local and Regional Government for the UK. He led a large city region on England's south coast and has an extensive corporate career - including founding and chairing FuturePlaces, a £3bn+ regeneration company. Now based in Dubai, he advises governments and investors across the Gulf.

He has spoken at and hosted dozens of conferences, including national government conferences. Organisers use him as a panellist, a chair and a moderator, on government and industry stages alike.

Topics

Phil speaks across the policy areas where government and private capital meet.

Government and delivery

How governments turn national strategy into results on the ground: devolution, city leadership and public service reform.

Cities, regeneration and infrastructure

Master plans residents actually notice, and the institutions that build and run them.

Capital and partnerships

Public-private partnerships that outlast the launch, and what shifts in UK policy mean for Gulf investors.

Health and care

Building health and care systems, and the workforce to run them.

Education

Schools, universities and the infrastructure, including housing, that brings students and teachers in and keeps them.

AI and digital government

What governments get right and wrong when they adopt AI, from someone who led a city's digital transformation.
