Phil is a Dubai-based adviser to governments and investors on urban delivery, infrastructure, public-private partnerships, health, education and UK-Gulf policy. He spent 15 years at the heart of UK government, attending Cabinet and serving as Leader of Local and Regional Government for the UK. He led a large city region on England's south coast and chaired FuturePlaces, one of the UK's largest urban regeneration companies, with a £3bn+ pipeline. He has worked closely with four Prime Ministers. An experienced speaker, chair and moderator, Phil has spoken at and hosted dozens of conferences, including national government conferences. He has appeared on the BBC, Sky, ITV and most major news broadcasters, and has been featured in the New York Times. He was appointed OBE for public service in 2026.
